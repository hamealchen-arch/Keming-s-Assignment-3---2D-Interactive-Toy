On your keyboard, hold spacebar/B to let it rain basketballs.
Tap the keys from 0-9 to increase/decrease the amount of basketballs that rain.
1 is the default; 5 is heavy rain; 9 is a whole storm; 0 is a "dribble".
The sun will grow/shrink in strength depending on what key you pressed, to indicate how many basketballs will rain.
Tap "Enter" on your keyboard to switch between day & night.
